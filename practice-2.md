# Practice - Part 2

We are going to make this README.md appear on your profile page.

- On the top menu to the far right, click on Settings
- Under repository name, replace `dd-example` with your GitHub account name
- On the far right, click `Edit README`
- Add some more details, such as a a table or image.
- When you've committed your changes, click on your icon in the upper right corner and click on Profile

You should see your README being rendered on your profile page.
